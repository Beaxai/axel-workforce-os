Rebuild the Cannabis WC Quote Flow with a two-phase structure:

PHASE 1 — FAST INDICATION (minimal info, instant pricing)
PHASE 2 — FULL PROPOSAL (complete UW info, triggered by user)

The goal is to get agents and prospects to a price indication 
as fast as possible with minimal friction. The full underwriting 
questionnaire only activates after the user reviews their 
indication and clicks "Request Proposal."

CRM STAGE MAPPING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Stage 1 — DRAFT
Trigger: User starts flow, saves and exits before 
completing Phase 1
Deal card shows: vertical, coverage type, business name 
if captured

Stage 2 — INDICATION
Trigger: User completes Phase 1 and views pricing 
indication screen
Deal card shows: indication range, total payroll, 
class codes, states

Stage 3 — PROPOSAL REQUESTED
Trigger: User clicks "Request Proposal" on indication 
screen
Deal card shows: indication range + "Full submission 
in progress"

Stage 4 — PENDING UW REVIEW
Trigger: User clicks "Submit for Approval" on final 
submission screen
Deal card shows: submitted date, PDF attached, 
assigned underwriter

Stage 5 — APPROVED / QUOTED
Trigger: Underwriter releases approved quote
(Handled in Pipeline — not part of this flow)

Stage 6 — BIND REQUESTED
Trigger: Client/agent requests bind
(Handled in Pipeline)

Stage 7 — BOUND
Trigger: Coverage bound, binder issued
(Handled in Pipeline)

FORM BEHAVIOR (ALL STEPS)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

- Sidebar visible and collapsible throughout entire flow
- Progress bar at top of content area on all steps

- Radio buttons: pink fill when selected
- Repeatable rows: pink outline + Add button, 
  trash icon to remove
- Currency: left $ prefix, comma-formatted
- Dropdowns: dark fill, pink on selection

GLOBAL STYLES:
- Base: #0a0a12
- Cards/surfaces: #13131f
