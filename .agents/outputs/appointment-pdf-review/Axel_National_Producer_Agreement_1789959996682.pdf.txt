AXEL WORKFORCE INSURANCE SOLUTIONS
NATIONAL PRODUCER AGREEMENT
THIS NATIONAL PRODUCER AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is made and entered into as of the date of its 
execution and acceptance by Airavata Group, LLC DBA Axel Workforce Insurance Solutions, a Delaware limited 
liability company ("Axel"), by and between Axel and the person or entity named on the signature page hereof 
("Producer"). Axel and Producer are referred to collectively as the "Parties." As used in this Agreement, "Axel" 
includes Airavata Group, LLC and its affiliates, where the context requires.
BACKGROUND
Axel operates as a wholesale insurance brokerage and program intermediary, providing retail producers with 
access to workers’ compensation insurance markets and Professional Employer Organization (PEO) and 
workforce solutions programs through Axel’s carrier, program partner, and service provider relationships. 
Producer desires to place workers’ compensation insurance and PEO/workforce solutions business through the 
insurers and program partners represented by Axel, utilizing Axel’s underwriting expertise, program access, 
and services. Axel may also facilitate access to additional insurance and non-insurance offerings from time to 
time. Axel is willing to make such insurance markets and workforce resources available to Producer for 
placement with admitted and/or non-admitted insurers and approved service providers, subject to the terms of 
this Agreement.
